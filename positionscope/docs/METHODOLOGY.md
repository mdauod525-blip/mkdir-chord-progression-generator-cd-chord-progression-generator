# Methodology

Every formula is implemented as a pure function under `src/lib/`. File references are given so each number can be checked independently.

## Data and freshness (`src/lib/data/service.ts`)

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| DEMO · simulated | Generated by `providers/demo.ts` for fictional `.DM` companies. Not market data. |
| End-of-day · date | Fetched from the provider during this request. Prices are as of that session's close. |
| Cached EOD · date | Served from the server cache. The fetch time appears in the tooltip and on the stock page. |
| STALE | Latest bar is more than 4 calendar days old, or a refresh failed and an older cached copy is shown. |
| Unadjusted | Provider series is not split/dividend adjusted (Alpha Vantage free tier). |

Nothing is real-time.

The demo generator is deterministic and **append-only**: each random stream consumes a fixed number of draws per day, so adding a day never changes earlier bars (`tests/data.test.ts`). Demo market holidays are not modelled.

## Indicators (`src/lib/indicators/index.ts`)

- **SMA(n)**: arithmetic mean of the last n closes.
- **EMA(n)**: k = 2/(n+1), seeded with SMA(n) at bar n−1.
- **RSI(14)**: Wilder smoothing of average gain and loss. Verified against the classic reference series.
- **MACD(12, 26, 9)**: EMA12 − EMA26; signal = EMA9 of MACD; histogram = MACD − signal.
- **ROC(n)**: (Cₜ / Cₜ₋ₙ − 1) × 100.
- **ATR(14)**: Wilder-smoothed true range.
- **Bollinger(20, 2)**: SMA20 ± 2 × population standard deviation.
- **Relative strength line**: (stock close / benchmark close), rebased to 100. RS over a period = stock % return − benchmark % return.
- **Weekly bars**: daily bars grouped by Monday-based week. A week counts as complete once a Friday (or later) bar exists. Weekly trend uses completed weeks only.

Each value at index i uses only bars ≤ i (tested by appending future bars).

## Trend (`src/lib/analysis/technical.ts`)

**Daily score**, starting from 0:
- close vs 200-day SMA: ±1
- 50-day vs 200-day SMA: ±1
- 50-day SMA slope over 20 sessions: ±1
- 200-day SMA slope over 20 sessions: ±0.5
- close vs 50-day SMA: ±0.5

The total is divided by 2, giving a range of −2…+2.
- ≥1.5 strong uptrend
- ≥0.5 uptrend
- >−0.5 sideways
- >−1.5 downtrend
- otherwise strong downtrend

**Weekly score** uses the same structure: close vs 40-week SMA, 10-week vs 40-week SMA, 40-week slope over 4 weeks, and close vs 10-week SMA. The total is divided by 1.75.

## Support and resistance (`src/lib/analysis/levels.ts`)

1. **Pivots.** A bar is a swing high if its high is strictly greater than the highs of k bars on each side (and the mirror for swing lows). k = 5 for daily bars over the last ~504 sessions, and k = 3 for weekly bars over the same window plus one year. A pivot only exists once k later bars exist, so this is look-ahead-free.
2. **Clustering.** Pivot prices are sorted and grouped greedily while within tolerance of the cluster mean. Tolerance = max(0.6 × ATR14, 1% of price). Zone = [min, max] of the cluster ± tolerance/4.
3. **Evidence.** Each zone records:
   - touches and weekly touches
   - wick rejections (a bar opens and closes on one side but trades into the zone)
   - role reversal (both swing highs and lows in the zone)
   - volume share (traded volume whose typical price fell inside the zone, out of the lookback total)
   - pivot volume relative to the average
   - first and last touch
4. **Strength (0–100)** = 9·min(touches, 6) + 8·weekly touches + 3·min(rejections, 6) + 12·role reversal + min(volume share %, 20) + 5 if pivot volume ≥ 1.3× average + recency (15 if under 60 days, 10 if under 180, 5 if under 365). Capped at 100.
5. **Selection.** Zones need ≥2 touches or a weekly pivot, and must lie within ±40% of price. The 8 strongest are kept. Role is support (below price), resistance (above price) or testing (price inside the zone).
6. **Secondary references.** 50/100/200-day SMAs with slope, and Fibonacci 23.6–78.6% of the 1-year high/low swing.

## Entry setups (`src/lib/analysis/setups.ts`)

Each candidate gets the following:
- **Targets:** the next resistance zones above the entry. If there are none, the 52-week high. If price is already above that, a labelled measured-move projection of the 3-month range.
- **Reward/risk:** (target₁ − entry mid) / (entry mid − invalidation).
- **Quality:** base + 6 × min(R/R, 4) − 6 × (number of reasons against).

The best candidate with R/R ≥ 1.2 and quality ≥ 40 is shown. Otherwise the result is **No clear setup**.

| Setup | Trigger (all daily) | Entry | Invalidation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pullback | Daily trend ≥ uptrend, close > 200-day SMA, within 1.5 ATR above a support zone (or within 1.2 ATR above the 50-day SMA), RSI < 62 | zone low → zone high + 0.5 ATR | entry low − 0.75 ATR |
| Breakout | Close crossed above a ≥2-touch zone in the last 5 bars and is ≤1.5 ATR above it | zone high → +1 ATR | zone low − 0.25 ATR |
| Retest | Zone broken by > 0.5 ATR within bars −45…−5, was below it in bars −90…−45, price now back within 0.75 ATR above it | zone low → zone high + 0.3 ATR | zone low − 0.6 ATR |
| MA recovery | Close crossed above the 50- or 200-day SMA in the last 5 bars after ≥15 of the prior 30 bars below it | MA − 0.25 ATR → MA + 0.75 ATR | min(20-bar low, MA − ATR) − 0.25 ATR |
| Relative strength | RS line at a 3-month high and improving, close > 50-day SMA, not extended | max(support high, close − ATR) → close + 0.25 ATR | support low − 0.5 ATR |
| Value + improving | Valuation score ≥ 65 and (MACD histogram rising and positive, or close > 50-day SMA) | support high → + 1 ATR | support low − 0.5 ATR |
| Reversal (higher risk) | Close < 200-day SMA and > 25% below the 52-week high, bullish RSI divergence or a MACD cross up, close > 20-day SMA | close − 0.5 ATR → close + 0.25 ATR | 20-bar swing low − 0.5 ATR |

**Context warnings** (shown, never used as standalone signals):
- **Extended:** (close − SMA50)/ATR > 4.
- **RSI ≥ 70 in an uptrend:** "strong momentum; not by itself a sell signal".
- **RSI ≤ 30 in a downtrend:** "oversold alone is not a buy signal".

## Fundamentals (`src/lib/analysis/fundamentals.ts`)

SEC EDGAR 10-K facts (`providers/sec.ts`):
- **Flow items** (revenue, income, cash flow) require a 350–380-day duration.
- **Balance-sheet items** use the fiscal-year-end instant value.
- When several tags cover the same period, the earlier tag in the preference list wins. Within one tag, the latest filing (including amendments) wins.
- Fiscal year = calendar year of the period end.

**Derived metrics:**
- **Margins** = item / revenue.
- **FCF** = operating cash flow − |capex|.
- **ROIC** = operating income × (1 − tax rate) / (debt + equity − cash). Tax rate = reported tax / pretax income, clamped to 0–35%. If either is missing, 21% is assumed and the assumption is shown.
- **EV** = market cap + debt − cash.
- **EBITDA** = operating income + D&A.
- **P/E** uses fiscal-year diluted EPS (not TTM). It is unavailable when EPS ≤ 0.
- **Trailing PEG** = P/E / 3-year EPS CAGR. It is shown only when that growth is > 0.

**Quality score:** the average of the available sub-scores:
- gross margin: 20→70%
- operating margin: 0→35%
- ROIC: 0→25%
- FCF margin: −5→25%
- net debt/EBITDA: 4→0
- 3-year operating-margin change: −5→+5 pp

**Growth score:** the average of the available sub-scores:
- revenue YoY: −5→25%
- revenue 3-year CAGR: −5→25%
- EPS YoY: −10→30%
- EPS 3-year CAGR: −10→30%

Each sub-score maps linearly from 0 at the first value to 100 at the second, clamped. Completeness = available inputs / total inputs.

## Valuation (`src/lib/analysis/valuation.ts`)

| Method | Fair value per share | Unsuitable when |
|---|---|---|
| Historical P/E | latest FY EPS × {min, median, max} of fiscal-year-end P/Es (close on or before each period end) | EPS ≤ 0, or fewer than 3 years overlap the price history |
| Historical P/S | latest revenue per share (current shares) × {min, median, max} historical P/S | shares missing, or fewer than 3 years |
| FCF DCF | 5 years at growth g, then Gordon terminal value. g = 3-year revenue CAGR capped at 15%. Scenarios (g×0.5, 10%, 2%), (g, 9%, 2.5%), (min(1.3g, 20%), 8.5%, 3%) | FCF ≤ 0, shares missing, no 3-year revenue history, or Financials sector |
| Peer P/E | EPS × sector-median P/E of the scanned universe × {0.85, 1, 1.15} | fewer than 3 peers with P/E, or EPS ≤ 0 |

**Blended** fair value = the median of the suitable methods for each scenario.

**Valuation score** = clamp(50 + 1.25 × base upside %):
- at most 20 if price is above the optimistic case
- at least 75 if price is below the conservative case
- at most 75 if base upside > 100% (flagged as a possible value trap or stale input)

**Assessment:** "Undervalued" above +15% upside; "Overvalued" below −15%.

## Opportunity score (`src/lib/scoring/score.ts`)

**Weights:** technical 25, quality 20, valuation 20, growth 15, momentum/RS 10, risk/reward 10. All are user-configurable and normalized.

**Components:**
- **Technical** = 0.5 × trend structure + 0.5 × best-setup quality (15 points if there is no setup). The trend structure is 60% daily and 40% weekly, each mapped from −2…+2 to 0…100. Subtract 10 if extended.
- **Momentum** = mean of: 3-month RS (−15→15), 6-month RS (−20→20), 63-day ROC (−15→25%), and MACD histogram sign (70 if positive, 35 if not). Subtract 10 if RSI > 78.
- **Risk/reward** = R/R mapped 0.5→0 and 4→100.

**Missing data.** For each component, adjusted = completeness × raw + (1 − completeness) × **25**. A fully missing component scores 25.

**Total** = Σ (weight × adjusted) / Σ weights. Completeness is the weighted average of component completeness: ≥80% is high confidence, ≥50% medium, otherwise low.

The score is a screening aid. It is **not** a probability of profit.

## Alerts (`src/lib/alerts/engine.ts`)

- Rules are evaluated on scan rows built from the latest daily bar.
- Weekly rules are evaluated only when the week is complete.
- Duplicate prevention: one event per rule per bar date.
- Cooldown: no new event within N calendar days of the last one.
- "Fundamentals changed" records a baseline on its first run and fires when a new fiscal year appears, or the quality score moves by ≥ the threshold.
- Watchlists with alerts on get implicit entry-zone and support-breakdown rules.

## Paper trading (`src/lib/paper/portfolio.ts`)

- An order stores `placedAfterBar`, the latest bar known when it was placed.
- **Market orders** fill at the open of the first later bar, ± slippage (bps), plus a fixed commission.
- **Buy limits** fill on the first later bar whose low ≤ limit, at min(open, limit). **Sell limits** mirror this. Limits expire after 20 sessions.
- Fills that exceed cash or the position are rejected.
- Accounting uses average cost, and commissions are included.
- Dividends are credited for shares held on the entered date.
- **Benchmark return** runs from the first fill date's open to the latest close. **Portfolio return** is simple, not time-weighted.

## Position sizing (`src/lib/risk/sizing.ts`)

- Risk per share = entry − stop + 2 × cost per share.
- Shares = floor(equity × risk% / risk per share), capped by equity (no leverage).
- **Warnings:**
  - exposure above the concentration limit
  - combined correlated exposure above 1.5 × the limit
  - risk above 2% of equity
  - R/R below 1.5
  - zero shares
- Stops can gap, so real losses can exceed the calculated maximum.
